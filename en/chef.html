Our kitchen

Alessandro, il Capitano in the kitchen

The symbol of our osteria is a galleon, and you will spot an old ship's wheel in the dining room. That is why everyone in the kitchen calls Alessandro il Capitano. He leads the team and cooks our traditional family recipes for you.

Ten years in the kitchen

Alessandro is 26 and has been working in kitchens since he was 16. For the past five years, he has been the head chef here at Corsetti. He specializes in various cooking techniques, using them every day to bring out the best in our Roman family recipes. Nothing here is pre-cooked: we prepare every dish to order, just like at home.
