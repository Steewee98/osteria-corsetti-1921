Roman cooking, the family way

We cook Roman dishes the way they're made at home. Nothing is precooked: every dish is prepared with patience. Sano guanciale, Pecorino Romano D.O.P. and a wine list with many labels.

A few dishes from our menu

Carbonara, amatriciana, gricia, spaghetti with garlic, oil, chili pepper and crunchy vegetables. In the full menu you will find prices and allergens for each dish.
